# Quant Research Platform

An end-to-end quantitative research and machine learning platform for researching financial markets, developing systematic trading strategies, evaluating portfolio risk, and turning research into production-oriented workflows.

## About

I am **Williams Dosunmu**, building this platform to combine quantitative finance, machine learning, artificial intelligence, data science, and software engineering into one deployable research system.

The platform is designed around the complete quantitative workflow:

**Market Data → Data Engineering → Research → Feature Engineering → Machine Learning → Strategy Development → Backtesting → Portfolio Construction → Risk Management → Evaluation → Deployment**

Rather than being a collection of isolated notebooks, the goal is to build a modular, reproducible and testable system that can take a research hypothesis from raw data through modelling, backtesting and evaluation.

## Architecture

1. **Data Layer** — acquire, store, clean and validate market data.
2. **Research Layer** — investigate factors, signals and statistical relationships.
3. **Feature Layer** — build reproducible predictive features.
4. **Modelling Layer** — train and evaluate statistical and ML models.
5. **Strategy Layer** — convert research signals into systematic strategies.
6. **Backtesting Layer** — simulate historical performance with realistic assumptions.
7. **Portfolio Layer** — allocation, optimisation and position sizing.
8. **Risk Layer** — volatility, drawdown, VaR, stress testing and risk analytics.
9. **Evaluation Layer** — compare experiments and analyse performance.
10. **Deployment Layer** — dashboards, APIs, containers and production workflows.

## Research Principles

- Reproducibility
- Out-of-sample evaluation
- Walk-forward validation
- Avoidance of look-ahead bias
- Transaction-cost awareness
- Risk-adjusted evaluation
- Modular architecture
- Automated testing
- Clear separation between research and production code
